# Motion Supply Brand OS Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 13, 2026_

Motion Supply Brand OS stores the minimum account and business information needed to provide the service. Account authentication is provided through Supabase. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe; Motion Supply Brand OS does not store full payment-card numbers.

## Data we process

We may process your account email, saved brands and SKUs, aggregate import summaries, Business Memory entries you choose to save, business snapshots, recommendation history, subscription status, and limited product-usage events. Raw Shopify customer rows are intended to remain client-side; only aggregate summaries are stored by the cloud service.

## Why we process it

We use this information to authenticate you, save and sync your workspace, calculate plan entitlements, provide business recommendations and historical context, operate billing, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, and understand product reliability and usage.

## Sharing and service providers

We use infrastructure and payment providers such as Supabase and Stripe to operate the service. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when legally required or when necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

## Retention and deletion

We retain account data while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for service, security, billing, dispute, and legal obligations. You may request account deletion from the product. Deletion is designed to remove the authentication account and user-owned application records through database cascades; payment records retained by Stripe may remain subject to Stripe's and applicable legal retention requirements.

## Security

We use access controls, row-level database security, authenticated APIs, request limits, secure transport in production, and restricted server credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices

You may use billing controls to manage a paid subscription. You may request deletion of your account and associated application data. For privacy, account, or support questions, use the support contact published in the app or on the Motion Supply Co. website.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material updates should be reflected by a new last-updated date.
